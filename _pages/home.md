I don't have a straight answer to where I'm from.

Born in Japan to a half French-Vietnamese father and a Malaysian-Singaporean mother, raised mostly in Singapore, now living in Canada.

I'm Chris. I run Growth at [NationGraph](https://nationgraph.com) in Toronto. I used to host events at [555](https://x.com/555studio_) in Montreal, bringing together people with all kinds of curiosities.

Before that I was a VC investor at [Panache Ventures](https://www.panache.vc/), an early-stage fund backing pre-seed and seed Canadian-rooted startups.

I also spent two years in the Singapore Military, attached to the [Singapore Artillery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Artillery) as a motor transport operator and an emergency ambulance operator.

When I'm not obsessing over startups I'm training BJJ (purple belt, somehow still addicted), logging miles for a marathon, supporting Chelsea through another season, buying soccer cards, or geeking out over watches.
